# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing professionals ensure product quality, validate acceptance criteria, and own the testing strategy. They collaborate with developers and product teams to define quality standards and prevent defects from reaching production.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Perform manual QA for feature acceptance before release
- Collaborate on automated testing strategy (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Manage security and compliance testing in the CI pipeline
- Track and report quality metrics (test coverage, defect rates)
- Participate in retrospectives to identify testing improvements

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Build confidence in release quality through comprehensive testing
- Enable fast, safe iteration through reliable automated tests

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement discussions
- Test plan reviews with product and development teams
- Quality dashboards and pre-release sign-offs
- Post-incident root cause analysis

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic direction and authority for product decisions. They align business goals with customer needs, own the product roadmap, and ensure projects deliver measurable customer and business value.

### Responsibilities
- Define product vision and strategy aligned with business objectives
- Approve project charters and success metrics before initiation
- Escalate and resolve cross-team dependencies and priority conflicts
- Review and validate product outcomes against success metrics
- Mentor Product Managers and provide guidance on trade-offs
- Communicate product strategy to stakeholders and executives

### Goals
- Maximize product-market fit and customer satisfaction
- Align delivery with business priorities and constraints
- Build a culture of data-driven decision making

### Typical Communication
- Initiation gate reviews (go/no-go decisions)
- Weekly PM sync and escalation discussions
- Monthly stakeholder and executive updates
- Product roadmap and release planning

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, resource allocation authority, and escalation resolution. They ensure projects align with organizational strategy and remove barriers to successful delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and resource allocation
- Provide business context and strategic constraints
- Resolve high-level escalations and priority conflicts
- Communicate project status to executive leadership
- Support risk mitigation and contingency planning
- Validate project outcomes against business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure investments deliver business value and ROI
- Reduce organizational friction and cross-team conflicts
- Maintain executive visibility and alignment on key initiatives

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation approvals
- Escalation resolution (Level 3 issues)
- Monthly executive status reports
- Release announcements and milestone celebrations

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security professionals ensure projects meet security and compliance requirements. They conduct threat modeling, security reviews, and incident response to protect customer data and organizational assets.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in design reviews for security implications
- Conduct security scanning and code review in CI/CD
- Define security acceptance criteria for releases
- Lead security incident response and post-mortems
- Maintain compliance with relevant standards (e.g., SOC 2, GDPR)
- Provide security guidance and best practices to development teams

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Build security into the development process rather than bolting it on
- Enable fast, confident releases through security automation

### Typical Communication
- Design and code review participation
- Security incident playbook and on-call coordination
- Compliance audits and attestation
- Security training and awareness programs
